# Product contract

## Promise

Circle is a privacy-first, local-first assistant for a deliberately small group of close friends. A user can add a shared moment or quick thought; Circle retains only the context they choose and may later offer a transparent opportunity to meet in person.

The north-star outcome is self-reported intentional in-person plans and meetups. It is not messages sent, reply speed, streaks, retention, or social-graph size.

## Core loop

1. A user adds a moment or captures a quick voice/text reflection.
2. Circle may present a person, time, place, or fact as a proposal. The user confirms or corrects it before it becomes a durable moment or memory.
3. The user reviews, edits, approves, or rejects each memory proposal.
4. The system considers only confirmed context and a user-chosen in-person cadence.
5. It presents one plain-language reason to consider meeting and an editable plan draft.

## Contractual boundaries

- A person is never ranked, scored, or labelled as a friendship-health risk.
- Prompts are explainable and can be adjusted, snoozed, dismissed, or disabled.
- The product never automatically records, messages, shares, imports contacts, changes a calendar, or claims that someone accepted a plan.
- A photo analysis result is disclosed as fixture, metadata, on-device recognition, or user selection. The current product only exposes a synthetic fixture adapter and has no photo-library or face-recognition access.
- Proposed facts cannot affect a prompt until a user approves them. Every confirmed fact retains a source note and can be edited, exported, forgotten, or deleted.
- The initial beta is one user, one city, at most ten active friends, and one opt-in event source. The long-term product limit remains intentionally small.

## Non-goals

Circle is not a CRM, social network, inbox assistant, dating tracker, mental-health assessor, relationship diagnosis tool, venue marketplace, payments system, group-coordination service, or social-discovery network.
