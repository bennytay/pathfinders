# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people whose privacy the product protects.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, or other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through the private security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this Code.

This Code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
