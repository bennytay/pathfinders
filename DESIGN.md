# Circle visual system

## Direction

Dark-charcoal mobile canvas, inspired by the quick, tactile feeling of Snapchat stories rather than a business app. A phone-sized shell is centered on larger screens.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#16151B`
- Surface: `#24222B`
- Ink: `#F7F5FF`
- Snap yellow: `#FFFC00`
- Hot pink: `#FF2D55`
- Cyan: `#00F0FF`
- Soft lavender: `#C9BAFF`

## Type

Use rounded Nunito with chunky weights for display copy and comfortable, legible body text.

## Components

- Rounded-full yellow primary buttons with near-black text.
- Sticker chips in high-contrast pastel fills.
- 2px dark outlines and offset shadows.
- Story-avatar rings that encode closeness with width and neon colour.
- Press states scale down slightly. Motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Layout

Single-column, mobile-first canvas. Bottom tab bar stays reachable, with the Log action elevated at centre. Large tap targets and airy stacked sections replace dense card grids.
